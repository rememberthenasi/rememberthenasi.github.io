The Month of Nissan · חודש ניסן

Nissan (also spelled Nisan) is the month of redemption: the month of the Exodus, of the Mishkan’s dedication, and of Pesach. The Torah calls it “the first of the months”. Here are its customs, its Chabad dates, and when everything falls in 5787 (2027).

About the month of Nissan

“This month shall be for you the head of the months” (Exodus 12:2): counting from the Exodus, Nissan is the first month of the Jewish year, even though the year number changes on Rosh Hashanah in Tishrei. It is also called Chodesh HaAviv, the month of spring, because Pesach must always fall in the spring.

Our Sages say, “In Nissan they were redeemed, and in Nissan they will be redeemed in the future.” The month is full of holy days: the dedication of the Mishkan on Rosh Chodesh, the twelve days of the princes’ offerings, and the festival of Pesach.

In the weeks before, Parshat HaChodesh (Exodus 12:1–20) is read on the Shabbat before, or on, Rosh Chodesh Nissan.

Customs of Nissan

The daily Nasi

From 1 to 13 Nissan, the passage of that day’s tribal prince is read from Numbers chapter 7, followed by the Yehi Ratzon. Read the guide or open today’s Nasi.

No Tachanun all month

Tachanun is not said for the whole of Nissan, and eulogies and public fasts are avoided, because most of the month is festive.

Birkat HaIlanot, the blessing on the trees

On first seeing fruit trees in blossom, we bless G-d “who left nothing lacking in His world, and created in it good creatures and good trees for people to enjoy”. In the Northern Hemisphere this is in Nissan; in the Southern Hemisphere, ask a rabbi when to say it.

Maot Chittim

Before Pesach, it is customary to give charity so that every family has matzah, wine and everything it needs for the festival (also called Kimcha DePischa).

Preparing for Pesach

The month is spent cleaning for Pesach, selling chametz through a rabbi, and getting ready for the Seder. See Chabad Pesach customs.

Chabad dates in Nissan

  • 2 Nissan: yahrzeit of the Rebbe Rashab, Rabbi Sholom DovBer Schneersohn (5680 / 1920), and the start of the leadership of the Frierdiker Rebbe, Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn.
  • 11 Nissan: birthday of the Lubavitcher Rebbe, Rabbi Menachem Mendel Schneerson (5662 / 1902). The Rebbe’s chapter of Tehillim from 11 Nissan 5787 is chapter 126.
  • 13 Nissan: yahrzeit of the Tzemach Tzedek, Rabbi Menachem Mendel of Lubavitch (5626 / 1866).

Each of these falls during the days of the Nasi: 2 Nissan, 11 Nissan and 13 Nissan.

Frequently asked questions

When is Rosh Chodesh Nissan 2027?

Rosh Chodesh Nissan 5787 is on Thursday, 8 April 2027, beginning at nightfall the evening before. It is always a single day, because the month of Adar before it always has 29 days.

Why is Tachanun not said in Nissan?

The first twelve days of Nissan were each a festival for the prince who brought his offering that day, followed by Erev Pesach, the eight days of Pesach and Isru Chag. Since most of the month is festive, Tachanun is left out for the whole month.

Is it Nissan or Nisan?

Both. The Hebrew is ניסן. “Nisan” follows the Hebrew spelling more closely, and “Nissan” follows the common pronunciation. They are the same month.

What are the Chabad dates in Nissan?

2 Nissan is the yahrzeit of the Rebbe Rashab and the start of the leadership of the Frierdiker Rebbe; 11 Nissan is the Lubavitcher Rebbe’s birthday; 13 Nissan is the yahrzeit of the Tzemach Tzedek.

What is Birkat HaIlanot?

A blessing said once a year on seeing fruit trees in blossom, thanking G-d for creating good trees for people to enjoy. In the Northern Hemisphere it is said in Nissan; in the Southern Hemisphere, ask a rabbi when to say it.

When is Pesach 2027?

Pesach 5787 begins at sundown on Wednesday, 21 April 2027 with the first Seder, and lasts until nightfall on Thursday, 29 April 2027 outside Israel (Wednesday, 28 April 2027 in Israel).
