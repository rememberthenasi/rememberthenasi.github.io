Six ways to remember the Nasi

Six redesign directions for the daily Nasi screen. Every phone below is live: tap the day controls and the Mark as said button. All of them show the same sample reading (Day 6, Elyasaf of Gad) so they can be compared fairly. Options B and C came from the first round, D to G are new. Please tell us which one you would most want to open every morning, and why.

Option BThe Dial

Remember the Nasi6 day streak

The 13 days form a ring you can tap. Filled means said and gold is today. It replaces the dropdown and makes the month feel like a path.

  • Friendliest for first-time visitors
  • Progress is visible at a glance
  • Nasi and Yehi Ratzon become tabs, so less scrolling

Option CPoster

RememberTheNasiStreak 06

Swiss grid meets Hebrew calligraphy. Black rules, huge numerals, one hot-pink signal. Thirteen cells are the whole navigation.

  • Boldest, most gallery-like
  • Fastest to use: one strip, one big action
  • Highest contrast, good for reading outdoors

Option DTekhelet Bath

Day 6 / 13Streak 6

קָרְבָּנוֹ קַעֲרַת־כֶּסֶף אַחַת שְׁלֹשִׁים וּמֵאָה מִשְׁקָלָהּ

His offering: one silver plate, its weight one hundred and thirty.

The whole screen is dipped a little deeper blue each day, like wool in the tekhelet bath. Day 1 is pale sky, day 13 is near black. The side ladder is the day picker.

  • You can tell the day by colour before reading
  • Calm, devotional, very little chrome
  • Text colour flips automatically for contrast

Option ECamp Map

The twelve leaders sit where their tribes camped around the Mishkan, three to a side, east at the top. Each day lights up one tribe. The thirteenth day is the Mishkan itself in the centre.

  • Evolves the Dial with real meaning
  • Teaches who the Nasi is each day
  • Blueprint line work, one gold accent

Nasi of Gad

The page of a Gemara: Hebrew in the centre with a ruled frame and a drop letter, English as a side gloss in small type, the day as the folio number. A rubric red appears once.

  • The day picker is a row of Hebrew letters
