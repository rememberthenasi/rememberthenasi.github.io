Chabad Pesach Customs · מנהגי פסח

Pesach (Passover) celebrates the Exodus from Egypt, “the season of our freedom”. Here are the customs kept in Chabad-Lubavitch, from the search for chametz to Moshiach’s Seudah on the last day, with the dates for Pesach 5787 (2027).

These are customs, not a full guide to the laws of Pesach. For practical questions, ask your rabbi.

Before Pesach

Maot Chittim

Charity before Pesach so that every family has what it needs for the festival.

Selling the chametz

Chametz that will not be destroyed is sold to a non-Jew through a rabbi before Pesach, and bought back afterwards.

Bedikat chametz, the search for chametz

On the night before Erev Pesach, the home is searched by the light of a candle, with a feather and a wooden spoon to gather any chametz into a paper bag. Following the Arizal, ten pieces of bread, each wrapped in paper, are put out around the house beforehand and found during the search. Afterwards Kol Chamira is said, cancelling any chametz that was not found.

Erev Pesach

Firstborn males fast, and usually take part in a siyum (the completion of a tractate) instead. The chametz is burned in the morning, and Kol Chamira is said again. After Mincha, Chabad custom is to recite the Seder Korban Pesach, the order of the Passover offering, printed in the siddur. Matzah is not eaten on Erev Pesach, so that it is eaten with appetite at the Seder.

Matzah and the Pesach kitchen

Hand-baked shmurah matzah

Chabad custom is to use hand-baked shmurah matzah, “guarded” from the time the wheat is harvested. The Zohar calls matzah “the food of faith” and “the food of healing”. The Rebbe’s matzah campaign encourages every Jew to have hand-baked shmurah matzah for the Seder.

No gebrochts

During Pesach, matzah is kept away from water and other liquids (gebrochts, or matzah shruya). Matzah stays covered on the table, liquids are poured carefully, and no matzah meal is cooked. On the last day of Pesach this is deliberately reversed, and gebrochts is eaten.

A careful kitchen

Many Chabad homes are especially stringent on Pesach, cooking from scratch, using few or no processed foods, and peeling fruit and vegetables.

The Seder

The Seder is held on the first night of Pesach, and outside Israel on the second night too. Before the Seder, Chabad say the full Hallel with a blessing at the evening service in the synagogue.

The Seder plate, according to the Arizal

Three shmurah matzot are placed under a cloth: called Kohen, Levi and Yisrael from top to bottom. On top of them:

  • Zeroa (roasted shank bone), top right
  • Beitzah (hard-boiled egg), top left
  • Maror (bitter herbs), centre
  • Charoset, below right
  • Karpas, below left
  • Chazeret (more bitter herbs, for the sandwich), bottom centre

Karpas is a raw onion or a boiled potato, dipped in salt water. Maror and chazeret are romaine lettuce and freshly grated horseradish.

The Haggadah

Many follow the Haggadah with the Rebbe’s commentary and customs, “Haggadah shel Pesach im Likutei Ta’amim, Minhagim uBiurim”, published by Kehot.

During Pesach

Counting the Omer

The Omer is counted each night from the second night of Pesach for forty-nine days until Shavuot. In the Chabad count, the day is said “laOmer”.

Shvi’i shel Pesach

The seventh day, 21 Nissan, is the anniversary of the splitting of the Sea. Many stay awake on the night before, learning Torah.

Acharon shel Pesach and Moshiach’s Seudah

The last day of Pesach (outside Israel, 22 Nissan; in 5787, Thursday, 29 April 2027) is connected with the future redemption: its Haftarah is Isaiah’s prophecy “A shoot will come forth from the stem of Jesse”, about Moshiach.

On this day gebrochts is eaten deliberately. In the afternoon comes Moshiach’s Seudah, the Feast of Moshiach, instituted by the Baal Shem Tov: a festive meal with matzah and four cups of wine, with stories and songs about the coming redemption. The Rebbe encouraged that it be held in every community.

Frequently asked questions

When is Pesach 2027?

Pesach 5787 begins at sundown on Wednesday, 21 April 2027 with the first Seder. Outside Israel it lasts eight days, until nightfall on Thursday, 29 April 2027; in Israel seven days, until nightfall on Wednesday, 28 April 2027.

What is gebrochts?

Gebrochts (Yiddish for “broken”), or matzah shruya in Hebrew, is matzah that has come into contact with water or another liquid, such as matzah balls or matzah dipped in soup. Chabad, like many chassidic communities, does not eat gebrochts on Pesach, out of concern that a trace of unbaked flour in the matzah could become chametz on contact with water. The custom is lifted on the last day of Pesach.

What is Moshiach’s Seudah?

A festive meal on the afternoon of the last day of Pesach, instituted by the Baal Shem Tov, celebrating the future redemption whose light shines on that day. Matzah is eaten and four cups of wine are drunk. The Rebbe encouraged that it be held in every community.

What is on the Chabad Seder plate?

Three matzot under a cloth, and on top of them, following the Arizal: the zeroa (shank bone) top right, the beitzah (egg) top left, the maror in the centre, the charoset below right, the karpas below left, and the chazeret at the bottom.

What do Chabad use for karpas and maror?

For karpas, a raw onion or a boiled potato, dipped in salt water. For maror and chazeret, romaine lettuce and freshly grated horseradish.

Do Chabad use hand-made or machine matzah?

Hand-baked shmurah matzah, watched from the time the wheat is harvested. The Rebbe’s matzah campaign encourages every Jew to have hand-baked shmurah matzah for the Seder.
